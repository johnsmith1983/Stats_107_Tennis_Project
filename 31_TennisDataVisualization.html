boxplot(

The Box plots show the median ace difference is slightly positive across all three court surfaces Grass and Hard courts have a median ace difference of close to 2 while clay courts have a median of about 1. Clay courts show a smaller spread in the middle 50% of matches as well especially compared with Grass and Hard. Every graph has some outliars but Grass seems to have much much less and the Grass is contained. This might suggest that the ace difference between winners and loser might vary depending on the surface of the court

boxplot(
  tennis_grass$w_ace,
  tennis_grass$l_ace,
  tennis_clay$w_ace,
  tennis_clay$l_ace,
  tennis_hard$w_ace,
  tennis_hard$l_ace,
  names = c(
    "Grass\nWinner",
    "Grass\nLoser",
    "Clay\nWinner",
    "Clay\nLoser",
    "Hard\nWinner",
    "Hard\nLoser"
  ),
  main = "Winner vs. Loser Ace Counts by Surface",
  xlab = "Court Surface and Match Outcome",
  ylab = "Number of Aces"
)

The boxplot shows that winners have higher median ace counts than losers across all three court surfaces. An interesting observation is that losing players seem to have more outliers, including some unusually high ace counts. This suggests that serving a large number of aces does not necessarily guarantee a match victory. The median differences are approximately 2 aces on grass, 1 on clay, and 2 on hard courts. These results suggest that winners tend to serve more aces, but ace counts alone do not determine match outcomes.

The summary table shows that winners serve more aces on average than losers across all three court surfaces. The largest difference occurs on grass courts, where winners serve approximately 2.05 more aces than losers. Hard courts show an average difference of approximately 1.77 aces, while clay courts show the smallest difference at approximately 0.91 aces. Grass courts also have the highest average ace counts, while clay courts have the lowest. This may reflect differences in playing conditions across surfaces. Overall, these preliminary results suggest that the association between ace counts and match outcomes may vary depending on court surface.
